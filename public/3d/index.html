How it works

Four steps, zero guesswork.

Every stage is verified by the machine before the next one runs. Nothing ships on hope.

1

Watch

Add Kick streamers to your watchlist. The radar tracks who is live and flags chat spikes the moment they happen.

2

Capture

Bring the moment in through an authorized route — native Kick clip, uploaded file, or your own OBS feed.

3

Cut & score

Trim frame-accurate, transcribe, and rank every candidate on a 100-point editorial rubric. Duplicates get rejected.

4

Render & approve

Vertical 1080×1920 with captions and your watermark, machine QA-checked, then approved by you. Nothing auto-publishes.
